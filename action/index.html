Take Action

What You Can Do

Sea level rise is a solvable problem — but it requires action at every level. Here's how you can make a difference, right here in San Diego.

Five ways to help

Where to start

01

Stay informed

Follow the science. Bookmark NOAA's sea level trends, read Scripps Institution updates, and track San Diego's Climate Action Plan progress. Knowledge is the foundation of effective advocacy.

02

Contact your representatives

Reach out to your City Council member, County Supervisor, and state legislators. Ask them to prioritize coastal resilience funding, support managed retreat policies, and accelerate the city's Sea Level Rise Adaptation Strategy.

03

Support local organizations

Volunteer with or donate to organizations working on coastal protection, climate policy, and community resilience in San Diego. Your time and resources amplify their impact.

04

Reduce your carbon footprint

Sea level rise is driven by climate change. Reducing emissions — through transportation choices, home energy, diet, and consumption — directly addresses the root cause. Every ton of CO₂ avoided matters.

05

Spread the word

Talk to your neighbors, share this site, and help others understand what's at stake for San Diego's coastline. Public awareness drives political will.
