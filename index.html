Local Impacts

What’s at stake for San Diego

Our Beaches

San Diego's iconic beaches — from Coronado to La Jolla — face accelerating erosion. Higher seas push storm waves further inland, shrinking sandy shorelines and threatening the coastal infrastructure millions rely on.

Learn more →

Mission Bay

Mission Bay's low-lying shores and recreational areas are among the most vulnerable in the region. Rising tides threaten parks, marinas, and the wildlife habitats that make this estuary a regional treasure.

Learn more →

Downtown Waterfront

The downtown waterfront, Embarcadero, and Barrio Logan face increasing flood risk as sea levels rise. Billions in infrastructure, housing, and economic activity sit within the projected flood zone.
