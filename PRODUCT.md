# Product context

## Purpose

Sea Level Rise San Diego is a public-interest educational website that helps San Diego residents understand sea level rise, see its likely local effects, and find practical ways to respond.

## Audience

- San Diego residents who want a clear introduction to sea level rise
- Coastal community members looking for place-specific impacts
- Students, educators, community groups, and local decision-makers

## Core journeys

1. Learn what causes sea level rise and how projections work.
2. Explore impacts on beaches, Mission Bay, downtown, and other local areas.
3. Find organizations, civic actions, and personal steps that can help.
4. Subscribe for updates or send a message.

## Brand commitments

- Scientifically grounded and locally specific
- Urgent without being alarmist
- Approachable, civic-minded, and action-oriented
- Coastal visual language using deep teal, coral, warm sand, and documentary imagery
- Editorial typography with DM Serif Display for headlines and Inter for interface and body copy

## Platform and delivery

- Responsive website for desktop and mobile
- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no build step
- GitHub repository: `rubywhite/sealevelrise`
- Netlify deployment, including Netlify Forms
